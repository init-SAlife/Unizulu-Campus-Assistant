Campus Navigator

Choose where you are starting from, your destination and how you travel (👣 Walk, 🚗 Car + walk or 🚙 Car only), then tap Get Route. The shortest route is shown first; tap Alternate route for other ways.

On the map, dark blue is driven and green is walked. P markers show the parkings: they appear when you zoom in, and a red one means a driver reported it full (whether there is space, you see when you get there). Found a parking full? Tap 🅿️ Full? Next parking: that parking is flagged and you get a new route from that parking to another one. Keep tapping at each full parking until you find a space, then tap ✓ Parked here.

With Current location, the arrow moves with you and tells you which buildings you are passing. If you go the wrong way the route turns red; if you take another path or turn back, a new route is found from where you are. Path blocked? Tap 🚧 Road closed.

You can also tap any place on the map to see it and get directions to it.
