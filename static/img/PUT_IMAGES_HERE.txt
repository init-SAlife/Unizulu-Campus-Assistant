Save two image files in this folder (static/img/):

  unizulu-logo.png   the UNIZULU logo (PNG with a transparent or white background works best)
                     shown in the page header and above the title on the sign-in page

  campus.jpg         a photo of the UNIZULU campus, landscape, about 1600 px wide or more
                     shown behind the sign-in form, under a blue tint

The names must match exactly (lower case). Until they are added, the pages
still work: the logo space is simply left out and the sign-in page is plain blue.
